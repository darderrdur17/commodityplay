The plan

12-Month Action Plan

Not vague milestones — specific knowledge targets and actions calibrated to where a serious candidate actually is, quarter by quarter.

Q1 · Months 1–3
Foundation — build the knowledge base. No applications yet.
  • Complete Chapters A–C of the Full Playbook. Be able to explain crack spreads, contango, and laytime unprompted before moving on.
  • Set up a daily reading habit: EIA Wednesday release plus one Platts or Argus headline per day. Calendar it.
  • Map the org structure of 3 target firms using LinkedIn and annual reports. Know who sits where.
  • Use the Function Matrix above to lock in your entry role. Don't hedge across three tracks — decide one and commit.
Q2 · Months 4–6
Positioning — build your story. Rewrite your resume. Start warm outreach.
  • Rewrite your resume using your archetype template from the Resume Templates module. Every bullet must demonstrate commercial awareness, not just activity.
  • Draft your market view hook — one specific, current observation about your target commodity. Practice saying it out loud.
  • Identify 10 people in target roles on LinkedIn. Start with informational conversations, not job asks.
  • If on the quant or analytics track: build one public data project involving commodity price data (EIA, CME, or AIS).
Q3 · Months 7–9
Active search — applications live. Interview pipeline building.
  • Apply to 3–5 roles per week. Prioritise firms with known junior hiring, not just brand names.
  • Practice 10 technical questions per week from the Interview Question Bank — focus on your target commodity's supply/demand, a trade idea walkthrough, and one supply disruption scenario.
  • Track every application in a pipeline with notes on which firms respond and at what stage. The pattern tells you where the filter is.
  • When the job board launches, use the role-specific openings to find live positions across Singapore, London, Hong Kong, and Tokyo.
Q4 · Months 10–12
Close — final-round prep and offer navigation.
  • For final rounds: prepare a 5-minute market view presentation on your target commodity. Show it unsolicited — it almost always lands.
  • Know your walk-away number before offer conversations begin. Understand total comp: base, discretionary bonus, and how the bonus is actually determined.
  • If no offer by month 12: diagnose systematically — resume stage, first interview, technical, or final. Each has a different fix. Don't apply more volume to a broken process.
